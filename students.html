01 Team jobs

Who does what?

Every team needs every job. You can have more than one job, and you can swap jobs during the season. Adults can teach you, but they can't do these jobs for you.

Team jobs: what each job does and who it suits
JobWhat you doYou might like this if you…WhenPick this job
Designer Plans how the robot will work. Draws ideas, measures, and helps pick the best one. love sketching and asking “what if?” All season
Builder Builds the robot and fixes it when something breaks. enjoy building and testing parts. All season
Coder Writes the code that makes the robot move and use its sensors. like puzzles and step-by-step thinking. All season
Notebooker Keeps the engineering notebook: what we tried, what happened, what we learned. like writing, drawing and noticing details. Every meeting
Strategist Plans how to score the most points. Watches other teams (scouting) and helps pick a plan with the partner. like games, maps and thinking ahead. Before and between Matches
Driver 1 Drives the first part of the Match, up to 35 seconds. Then hands the controller to Driver 2. stay calm and like fast, careful driving. In the Match: 1:00 → 0:35
Driver 2 Takes the controller between 0:35 and 0:25 and drives until the buzzer. are ready to jump in and finish strong. In the Match: 0:25 → 0:00
Loader Puts Bean Bags into your Load Zone, one at a time, flat on the floor. Red and blue bags go in the matching color zone. Hands stay out of the field. are quick, careful and good at timing. In the whole Match. The Driver who isn't driving can be the Loader too.

Tap “That's me” on the jobs you want to try. Your picks are saved on this device.

The Drive Team

In a Match, each team sends up to 3 students to the Driver Station: Driver 1, Driver 2 and a Loader. Only they may be there.

Trade jobs!

You can use different Drivers and Loaders in different Matches. Many teams rotate so everyone gets a turn. Just decide as a team.

03 Inspection

Ready for the inspector?

Before you can play any Match, an inspector checks that your robot follows the rules. If something isn't right, you fix it and come back. It's normal, so don't worry!

04 Better robot

Make it better, one step at a time.

Great teams don't build a perfect robot the first time. They test, fix one thing, and test again. Pick one idea below for your next practice.

  1. Test itRun the same test the same way.
  2. Find the problemWhat went wrong, exactly?
  3. Change ONE thingOnly one, or you won't know what helped.
  4. Test againDid the number get better?
  5. Write it downNotebook time! Numbers and a picture.

Grab one bag, every time

Your robot can only hold or push one Bean Bag at a time. A strong, simple grabber beats a fancy one.

Try this Pick up 20 bags. Count the misses. Fix the biggest problem first.

SG6

Reach higher Goals

L1 is 3 points, L2 is 6, L3 is 12 and L4 is 16 (yellow bags only). Your robot can grow taller after the Match starts.

Try this Find the highest level you can score 10 times in a row.

SG3SC4SC5

Stretch, but stay legal

Start at 11″ × 20″ × 15″. After the start you may stretch to 24″ long, but never wider than 11″.

Try this Raise your arm slowly next to a tape measure. Does any part go past the limits?

R3SG2

Drive straight, turn fast

More trips means more points. A robot that drifts wastes time. Good wheel grip and a Gyro Sensor can help.

Try this Drive 6 feet, 5 times. Measure how far it drifts left or right each time.

Don't tip over

If the robot tips over or gets stuck, you must reset it, and any bag it was holding is gone for the Match.

Try this Put heavy parts low. Drive fast, then stop. Does it rock?

GG10

Let the bag go

After the Match, bags must come off easily, even with the robot turned off.

Try this Turn the robot off. Can one person take the bag out with one hand?

R5

Fix things fast

It's smart to keep the battery and power button easy to reach, and the Brain screen easy to see.

Try this Time a battery swap. Can you do it in 30 seconds?

R7

Shorter trips, more bags

Most bags have to cross the whole field. Some paths are long and open, others are short and tight. A smaller robot can use the shortcuts.

Try this Time one full trip: pick up, drive, score, come back. Change one thing. Time it again.

Stay legal while you upgrade

No more than 6 motors, only official VEX IQ parts (plus a few allowed extras), and nothing 3D printed. After a big change, ask to be inspected again. Check the inspection checklist before you go to the field.

Rules are marked with their official numbers and link to the plain-English rules page. Try this ideas are suggestions from this guide, not rules.
