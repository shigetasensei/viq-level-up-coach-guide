Cheat sheet What the timer calls

Know the calls.

The timer follows the Match rules, so your Drive Team can practice with the same calls they'll hear at the field. At an event, the field timer and the referee are always in charge.

Timer calls by time remaining

Autonomous Skills

There's no controller and no hand-off, so the timer only calls the last ten seconds and the buzzer. Start the run from the Brain, not the controller.

RSC6RSC7

Stop early

Pressing Stop early records a time. In Skills it's the Skills Stop Time, rounded up to the next second, used only as a tiebreaker. In a Teamwork Match it's the Match Stop Time for a first-place Finals tiebreaker, rounded down to an even number.

RSC8GG13T14

Practice tips

  • Pick a fixed moment, like 0:30, to pass the controller.
  • Prop your phone where the whole Drive Team can see it, and use Full screen.
  • Turn on Voice calls if nobody is looking at the screen.

Browsers only play sound after you press a button, so tap Start once to enable it. Some phones are silent when the ringer is off; the screen colors and vibration still work. The screen stays on while the timer runs, on browsers that allow it.
