Event schedule planner

See what a tournament day looks like for your team: when you play, who your partners are, how long you rest between matches, and when to fit in Robot Skills runs.

Practice tool. The official schedule comes from the Event Partner's Tournament Manager. T8

1. Set up your event

Either generate a practice schedule from a team list, or import the real match list once your Event Partner publishes it. All timings are editable, because cycle times, lunch and Skills hours vary by event.

Teams

Team 1 is the red team and Team 2 the blue team (rule GG1). Time may be 12-hour (9:06 AM) or 24-hour (13:05). Copying rows from a spreadsheet works too.

Click any team number in the Field board to add or remove it.

Matches

Local qualifiers run at least 6 Qualification Matches per team and Championship events at least 8 (T9).

Day plan

Each team gets exactly three Driving and three Autonomous Coding Skills runs (T15). Finals Alliances pair the ranked teams in order and play one at a time (T14).
