Plan the day: event schedule plannerPlan the day.

See when your team plays, who your partners are, how long you rest between Matches and where Robot Skills runs fit, before event day arrives.

Practice tool. Your Event Partner's Tournament Manager makes the official schedule. T8

01 Set up your event

Teams, timing, go.

Generate a practice schedule from a team list, or paste the real match list when your Event Partner publishes it. Every timing is editable, because cycle times, lunch and Skills hours vary by event.

Teams

Team 1 is the red team and Team 2 the blue team (rule GG1). Time may be 12-hour (9:06 AM) or 24-hour (13:05). Copying rows from a spreadsheet works too.

Click any team number in the Field board to add or remove it.

Matches

Local qualifiers run at least 6 Qualification Matches per team and Championship events at least 8 (T9).

Day plan

Each team gets exactly three Driving and three Autonomous Coding Skills runs (T15). Finals Alliances pair the ranked teams in order and play one at a time (T14).
